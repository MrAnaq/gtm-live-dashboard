## 4. Copy Direction (for copywriter — run full brief analysis per cold-outreach skill)

- **Awareness stage:** Problem Aware. They know missed calls / empty pipeline hurt. They do not know a fixed-price install exists (everything they've been pitched is a retainer or a SaaS subscription).
- **LF8 driver:** Segment 1 → #3 (fear of loss — every missed call is a job that called the next guy). Segment 2 → #3 + #6 (pipeline fear + losing deals to competitors who out-prospect them).
- **Core pain, Segment 1:** missed calls cluster exactly when you're busiest — the money leaks when you can least see it.
- **Core pain, Segment 2:** founder-led outbound that stops every time delivery gets busy — feast-or-famine on a loop.
- **Differentiation angle (both):** one person builds it, fixed price, you own it, no retainer hostage-taking. "Built and operated by one person" is the brand line — use it.
- **Never mention "AI" first** in Segment 1 copy or calls. The outcome (answered calls, booked jobs) leads; the mechanism is a footnote.
- Sequence: standard 4–5 touch per skill. Breakup email gets real weight — highest reply rate in our data.
- Incentive test: hold gift cards for the B2B segment A/B only. Local owners respond to the free "missed-call audit" (reciprocity via diagnosis, not gift).
