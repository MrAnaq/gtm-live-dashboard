## Funnel math (assumptions labeled)

- **Call track (highest confidence):** 20 dials/day × 10 working days = 200 dials → at 15–20% connect ≈ 30–40 owner conversations → at ~10% book ≈ 3–4 meetings. This track alone can carry the goal if email underperforms.
- **Email track:** 1,500 verified contacts × 4–5 touches ≈ 6,000–7,000 sends across 4 weeks — *if* warm capacity supports ~250–300 sends/day. At 1–2% positive rate → 15–30 positives → 8–15 meetings. **The sends/day number is the one open variable — it comes from the warm-capacity count Adam supplies at this gate.** If capacity is lower, the send window stretches and the call track carries more weight; we launch either way (brief §6: don't wait on warm-up).
- **Close:** 12–15 meetings → 2–3 closes at a 20% close rate on a $2,500 fixed-scope, problem-aware pitch with existing proof assets.
